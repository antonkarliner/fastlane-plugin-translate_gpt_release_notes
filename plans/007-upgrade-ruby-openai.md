## Why this matters

`ruby-openai` is pinned at `~> 3.7` (locked at 3.7.0, released 2023); the current major is 8.x. Five majors of drift means the plugin can't use newer endpoints, parameters, or model behaviors, and the gap will only widen — eventually the old gem stops working against the live OpenAI API. The OpenAI provider's surface is small (`OpenAI::Client.new(access_token:, request_timeout:)` and `client.chat(parameters:)`), and that surface has been stable across major versions, so the upgrade is likely low-friction — but "likely" is why this is a spike with a real verification gate, not a blind bump. The goal is to land on a current, supported major with the test suite and the OpenAI contract test (plan 005) green.

- The response still responds to `dig('choices', 0, 'message', 'content')` (Hash-like).
